# RubyEvents

- **Status:** Manual / enrichment only; low-volume monitoring.
- **Official/source URL(s):** [RubyEvents CFP](https://www.rubyevents.org/cfp); separate [Ruby Conferences YAML](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ruby-conferences/ruby-conferences.github.io/main/_data/conferences.yml).
- **Interface:** Public CFP web page; no established structured export.
- **Verification:** Point-in-time live snapshot dated 2026-11-11.

## Data and access

RubyEvents showed two live calls in the snapshot. The page can support occasional human checks for Ruby speaking opportunities, but the review established neither a structured export nor a CFP-data license. Do not infer a reusable bulk-data interface from the public page.

## Caveats

RubyEvents is distinct from the older Ruby Conferences YAML dataset. The latter had different freshness and licensing findings and must not be presented as RubyEvents' export or as evidence of current RubyEvents calls. The two-call result is a snapshot, not a guarantee of continuing volume.

## Integration guidance

Keep RubyEvents out of scheduled feed ingestion. Use low-volume manual checks, open the linked call for current details, and create or enrich a note only after verifying its deadline and submission link. Reassess licensing and any documented export before proposing automation.

## Evidence

The compact additional-sources review recorded two live calls, no established export, and no established CFP-data license on 2026-11-11. Its access-block findings separately identified the older Ruby Conferences YAML as stale.
